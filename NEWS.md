## 1. Tankers ablaze in Hormuz — two oil tankers hit naval mines

Two oil tankers struck naval mines and exploded and caught fire in the
Strait of Hormuz early on Wednesday 25 November, Iran's Islamic
Revolutionary Guard Corps said; the vessels had been attempting to
transit an unauthorised route through the strait. The same day,
Yemen's Houthis said they had attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil
refinery, saying the attack caused fires.

## 2. Trump's new tariffs — duties proposed on 14 trading partners

The Trump administration on Wednesday proposed new tariffs on 14 major
trading partners to combat what it calls structural excess industrial
capacity, after an eight-month Section 301 unfair-trade probe. China
faces the highest capped rate, 20% — rising to 40% if the US–China
trade truce unravels; the other partners include the EU, Japan, South
Korea, India and Indonesia. Mexico and Norway are excluded for now.
The duties face a public comment period and a hearing, pushing any
start into January.

  partners to fight excess factory capacity"
- Reuters, 25 Nov 2026: "Trump announces new tariffs, undeterred by
  midterms defeat"

## 3. Huawei convicted — US jury finds Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts

A federal jury in Brooklyn found Chinese telecoms giant Huawei guilty
on 10 of the 12 criminal counts it faced — conspiracy to steal trade
secrets, bank and wire fraud, sanctions violations and money
laundering — after an 11-week trial, and acquitted it of the lead
racketeering charge and of obstructing justice. Sentencing is set for
September 2027. The verdict was delivered on Tuesday 24 November in
New York and reported worldwide on 25 November (Reuters dateline 25
November; The Register published 25 November 06:16 UTC).

- Reuters, 25 Nov 2026: "China's Huawei convicted in sprawling US

## 4. Pakistan's first astronaut — Shenzhou-24 docks at Tiangong

China's Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship launched from Jiuquan at 00:18
UTC on Wednesday 25 November and docked with the Tiangong space
station at 03:56 UTC, about three and a half hours later. Aboard were
commander Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan
Ali — the first Pakistani to reach orbit and the first foreign
astronaut to visit Tiangong. Ali, a payload specialist, spends about
a week aboard before returning on Shenzhou-23.

## 5. CIA and ODNI hacked — spy agencies' unclassified network breached

Hackers breached an unclassified computer network used by the CIA and
the Office of the Director of National Intelligence to analyse
open-source intelligence, exposing some unclassified information; the
National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency was hit at the same time.
Officials stressed no classified systems were affected and the issue
was resolved. Early indications suggest the hack may have originated
in China, and investigators suspect the AI hacking tool ARTEX was
used. The New York Times first reported the breach on Tuesday 24
November (US time); it was reported worldwide through 25 November.

  hacked"
